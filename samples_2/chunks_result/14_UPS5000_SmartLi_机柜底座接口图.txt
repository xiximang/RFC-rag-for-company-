技术要求/Technical requirements:
1.机柜宽度尺寸公差（0，-3）mm，图中标注尺寸单位：毫米 1. The cabinet width tolerance is (0, –3) mm. The unit of the dimensions in the figure is millimeter.
2.底座材料不做要求，颜色建议为黑色（RAL9005）
2. There is no requirement on the base material. The recommended color is black (RAL9005).
3.底座承重要求不少于2T；
3. The bearing capacity of the base must be at least 2 tons.
4.相连底座需用螺栓并联，底座需保证至少四角与地面固定
4.Connect adjacent bases in parallel using bolts .Ensure that each base is secured to the ground through at least four corners.
5.与机柜连接位置需焊接M12螺母，且要保证焊接强度，不可出现虚焊；
5. Weld M12 nuts at the positions where the cabinet is connected to the base. The welding strength must be ensured and all nuts are properly welded.
